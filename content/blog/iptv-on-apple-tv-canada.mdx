## Setting up IPTV on Apple TV, step by step

### Step 1 — Get your trial login

Message **IPTV Canada** on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/212710141872) and ask for a free 24-hour trial. You'll get either:
- An **M3U URL** (a link ending `.m3u` or `.m3u8`), or
- **Xtream Codes** details: server URL, username and password

Either works with the apps above.

### Step 2 — Install your app

On the Apple TV:
1. Open the **App Store**
2. Search for **IPTV Smarters Pro** (or your preferred app)
3. Install it

### Step 3 — Add your subscription

**In IPTV Smarters Pro:**
1. Open the app
2. Choose **Add User**
3. Pick your login type:
   - **Xtream Codes API** — enter server URL, username, password
   - **M3U URL** — paste the link
4. Select **Add User** — channels load in 30–60 seconds

**In GSE Smart IPTV:**
1. Open the app
2. Go to **Remote Playlists**
3. Tap **+**
4. Name it and paste your M3U URL or Xtream Codes details
5. Tap **Add**

### Step 4 — Add the guide (EPG)

Your IPTV Canada subscription includes an EPG URL. Add it in the player's settings for:
- Now-and-next across all channels
- A 7-day guide
- Channel logos and descriptions

### Step 5 — Tidy up your channels

Most apps let you:
- Filter by category (Canadian, Sport, News, Entertainment, Films, Kids)
- Save favourites for the channels you watch most
- Sort alphabetically or by category

## Troubleshooting on Apple TV

**Slow to load or buffering**

Check your speed first — 10 Mbps minimum for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K. Apple TV is at its steadiest on a wired ethernet connection via the adapter; Wi-Fi is fine for most streams but ethernet rules out network wobble.

**App missing from the Canadian App Store**

Some IPTV apps don't appear in the Canadian App Store. Creating a free Apple ID set to another region (such as the US) opens up the full tvOS store — a common workaround that doesn't affect anything else on your Apple TV.

**Channels won't load after setup**

Refresh the playlist in the app's settings. If your login was issued moments ago, give it a few minutes and retry. Still stuck? Message IPTV Canada on WhatsApp — replies usually come within the hour.
