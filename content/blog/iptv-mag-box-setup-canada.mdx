## What a MAG box actually is

A MAG box is a set-top box made by Infomir for one job: IPTV. Where a Firestick or Android box runs a general-purpose operating system, a MAG box runs a lightweight Linux system built entirely around IPTV, which is why it tends to feel snappier and crash less for live TV.

The MAG models you'll see most in Canada:

- **MAG 322 / 322W** — entry-level, dependable for HD
- **MAG 420 / 420W** — handles 4K UHD, quicker processor
- **MAG 524 / 524W3** — top of the range, 4K HDR, dual-band Wi-Fi

## Setting up a MAG box for IPTV Canada, step by step

### Step 1 — Connect the box

1. Plug the MAG box into your TV with the HDMI cable
2. Connect to your router by ethernet (steadier) or set up Wi-Fi in Settings
3. Power it on and let it boot — the first boot takes about 30–60 seconds

### Step 2 — Enter the portal URL

The MAG box reaches your service through a **portal URL**, which your provider gives you when you subscribe (you'll usually send them the box's MAC address first so they can authorise it).

1. On the home screen, open **Settings**
2. Go to **System Settings → Servers → Portals**
3. In **Portal 1 URL**, type the portal URL from your provider
4. In **Portal 1 Name**, put any label (e.g. "IPTV Canada")
5. Press **OK** to save

### Step 3 — Reboot and load

1. Return to the main menu and choose **Reboot**
2. The box restarts and connects to the portal on its own
3. The full channel list appears — allow 1–2 minutes on the first load

### Step 4 — Find your way around

Once it's loaded:
- Use the **remote** to move through categories (Sport, Entertainment, News, Kids)
- Press **OK** to open a channel
- Use the **EPG button** for the programme guide
- Press **Back** to return to the list

## Fixing common MAG box issues

### Portal won't load

- Re-check the portal URL — a single wrong character breaks it
- Confirm your internet is actually up
- Reboot both the MAG box and the router
- Ask your provider to confirm the portal URL is still active

### Buffering or freezing

- Swap Wi-Fi for ethernet for a steadier link
- Check your speed — 25 Mbps or more for 4K
- Ask your provider on WhatsApp whether there's an alternative server URL

### No channels showing

- Your subscription may have lapsed — check with your provider
- Refresh the portal: Settings → System Settings → Servers → Portals → reboot
