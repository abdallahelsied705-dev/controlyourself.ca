## What to test in your 24 hours

Spend the time on the things that actually matter:

### 1. A sports channel mid-match

The toughest test there is. The main sport channels should load instantly in HD or 4K with no stutter. Hold steady on a live match and it'll hold on anything.

### 2. The main Canadian entertainment channels, live

These are the baseline. If they aren't flawless, nothing above them will be.

### 3. Stability over 20-plus minutes

Plenty of services look fine for the first few minutes then sag. Watch something for a full 20 — a news bulletin, a half of football, a film — and look for buffering, freezing or quality drops.

### 4. The guide (EPG)

The 7-day guide should load on its own and show schedules for the major Canadian channels. A missing or broken guide points to a neglected service.

### 5. On demand — search for something specific

Look up a particular film or series in the library and check it opens quickly, in HD, without interruptions.

### 6. On your actual device

Always test on the device you'll use daily. A stream that's fine on your phone can behave differently on an older Smart TV — the trial lets you confirm that in advance.
