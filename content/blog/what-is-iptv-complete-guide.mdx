**IPTV** is short for **Internet Protocol Television** — television that travels to you over your broadband line instead of through a rooftop aerial, a satellite dish or a cable in the wall. The picture arrives much like it does on YouTube or a film app, except IPTV also gives you proper live channels with a programme guide, not only on-demand clips.

This guide walks through what IPTV is, how it reaches your screen, what you need to run it in Canada, roughly what it costs, where it stands legally, and how to get going.

## How IPTV actually works

Old-fashioned TV is *broadcast*: one signal goes out and every aerial or dish grabs it at the same moment. IPTV is *delivered on request*. The instant you pick a channel, your app asks the provider's server for that stream, and the server sends it to your device in a steady run of small data packets that the app stitches back into moving pictures.

Day to day, it goes like this:

1. **You take out a subscription** with an IPTV service.
2. **The provider sends you a login** — normally an **M3U playlist link** or an **Xtream Codes** login (a server address, a username and a password).
3. **You drop that login into an IPTV app** such as TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro or GSE Smart IPTV.
4. **The app pulls in the channel list and TV guide**, and you choose what to watch.

Because it all runs inside an app, the one subscription works on the living-room TV, a phone, a tablet or a laptop — wherever that app will install.

## The three kinds of IPTV content

- **Live TV** — channels streamed as they go out, with an electronic programme guide (EPG) showing what's on now and next.
- **Catch-up** — programmes from roughly the last seven days that you can start after they've aired.
- **Video on demand (VOD)** — a library of films and box sets you begin whenever you like, exactly like a streaming app.

## What you need to watch IPTV in Canada

| What you need | Minimum | Better |
|---------------|---------|--------|
| Broadband speed | 10 Mbps for HD | 25 Mbps+ for 4K |
| Device | Smart TV, Firestick, Android box, phone, tablet, PC or MAG box | Firestick 4K or Android TV on ethernet |
| App | Any M3U / Xtream Codes player | TiviMate (Firestick/Android) or IPTV Smarters Pro |
| Subscription | Your chosen provider | One with a trial and real support |

There's no dish, no engineer and no long contract involved. If your TV is older and has no app store, a Firestick in a spare HDMI port turns it into an IPTV-ready set in a couple of minutes.

## Is IPTV legal in Canada?

The technology itself is perfectly legal — Canadian broadcasters lean on exactly the same internet-streaming method for their own catch-up apps. What decides legality is whether the *content* is properly licensed. Licensed services are fine; unlicensed streams of copyrighted channels are not. Before paying anyone, check they publish clear terms of service and a route to report copyright concerns (a DMCA policy).

## The jargon, decoded

- **M3U link** — a single web address that holds your whole channel list; most apps accept it.
- **Xtream Codes** — a login (server, username, password) that also loads the TV guide and VOD for you.
- **EPG** — electronic programme guide, i.e. the listings grid.
- **Connections** — how many devices can stream at once on one subscription.
- **Buffering** — those pauses while the stream catches up, nearly always down to Wi-Fi or slow broadband. See our [buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a special box for IPTV?**
No. A Smart TV, a Firestick, an Android box, a phone or a laptop all do the job. MAG boxes are purpose-built IPTV boxes, but they're optional.

**Can I watch on more than one TV?**
Yes, as long as your subscription includes more than one connection. Each device streaming at the same time uses one connection.

**Does it work over Wi-Fi?**
Yes, though a wired ethernet cable is steadier, particularly for 4K and live sport.

**Can I use IPTV abroad?**
Yes. Because it runs over the internet, it works anywhere you have a stable connection.
