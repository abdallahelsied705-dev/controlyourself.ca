- Each pass only covers **that broadcaster's slice** of the fixtures
- **No 4K** on most standard passes (an upgrade costs more)
- At C$30–C$35 it's roughly **four times** the cost of IPTV Canada for fewer matches

**Best for:** casual fans who only want one broadcaster's matches.
