## Setting up IPTV on Android TV, step by step

### Step 1 — Get your free trial

Message **IPTV Canada** on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/212710141872) and ask for a free 24-hour trial. Your Xtream Codes details — server URL, username and password — arrive within minutes. No card needed.

### Step 2 — Install TiviMate

TiviMate is the best player for Android TV, straight from Google Play:

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android TV
2. Search **TiviMate IPTV Player**
3. Install the free version (a Companion subscription unlocks the premium extras)
4. Open TiviMate and select **+**

### Step 3 — Add your subscription

In TiviMate choose **Add Playlist → Xtream Codes API** and enter:
- **Server URL** — supplied with your trial
- **Username** — from your IPTV Canada login
- **Password** — from your IPTV Canada login

Select **Next** and give it 30–60 seconds while your 50,000+ channels load.

### Step 4 — Add the guide (EPG)

Once channels are in, go to **Settings → Playlists → EPG Sources** and add the EPG URL from your subscription. That brings in the full 7-day guide for the Canadian channels, just like a satellite box.

### Step 5 — Browse and watch

Use the **Channels** tab to move through Canadian, Sport, Entertainment, News and Kids, and drop the ones you watch most into **Favourites** for quick access.
