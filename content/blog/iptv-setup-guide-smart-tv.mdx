Most Smart TVs from Samsung, LG, Sony, Philips, TCL and Hisense will play IPTV with no extra hardware — all you need is the right app and your login. The app depends on your TV's operating system:

| TV brand | Operating system | Recommended apps |
|----------|------------------|------------------|
| Samsung | Tizen | IPTV Smarters Pro, SET IPTV, Smart IPTV |
| LG | webOS | IPTV Smarters Pro, SET IPTV, Smart IPTV |
| Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense (Android / Google TV) | Android TV | TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro |
| Other / older TVs | Varies | Add a Firestick or Android box |

Before you start, have your **Xtream Codes** login (server, username, password) or **M3U link** to hand, and get the TV online — ideally on an ethernet cable.

## Samsung and LG: IPTV Smarters Pro

Where it's in your TV's store, IPTV Smarters Pro is the easiest route because it signs in with Xtream Codes directly.

1. Open **Samsung Apps** or the **LG Content Store** and search **IPTV Smarters Pro**.
2. Install and open it, then pick **Login with Xtream Codes API**.
3. Enter any name, then the **username, password and server URL** from your provider.
4. Your Live TV, Movies and Series sections load in under a minute.

## Samsung and LG: SET IPTV or Smart IPTV (playlist upload)

These load your playlist through their website using the TV's MAC address.

1. Install **SET IPTV** or **Smart IPTV** from your TV's store and open it.
2. Note the **MAC address** on screen.
3. On your phone or computer, go to the app's official website, enter the MAC address and paste your **M3U link** (and EPG link if you have one).
4. Restart the app on the TV — the channels appear on their own.

Both apps give a short free period and then charge a small one-off activation fee on their website.

## Android TV and Google TV (Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense)

Android TVs run the same apps as a Firestick, so use **TiviMate** for the best TV-style feel or **IPTV Smarters Pro** for a quick free setup.

1. Open **Google Play** on the TV and install **TiviMate** or **IPTV Smarters Pro**.
2. Choose **Add playlist → Xtream Codes** and enter your server, username and password.
3. Load the EPG when prompted so the guide fills in.

Our [Android TV guide](/blog/iptv-android-tv-canada) has the detailed TiviMate settings.

## Older TVs, or TVs without a good app

If your set is more than about six years old or its store has no reliable IPTV player, the simplest fix is a **Firestick** or **Android TV box** in a spare HDMI port. It costs less than a single month of a satellite package and opens up the best apps. See our [Firestick app comparison](/blog/best-iptv-apps-firestick).

## Getting the best picture on a Smart TV

- **Use ethernet** over Wi-Fi — the biggest single improvement.
- **Match the quality to your line:** 4K channels want ~25 Mbps; drop to FHD if they stutter.
- **Turn off motion smoothing** in the picture settings for sport if it causes artefacts.
- **Keep the app updated** from your TV's store.

## Troubleshooting

**Channels won't load:** check the login for typos, confirm the subscription is active, and restart the app. In Smart IPTV, hold the back button to force a playlist reload.

**No TV guide:** add the EPG link in the app's settings and give it a few minutes to download.

**Picture freezes:** go wired, restart the router, and lower the channel quality — our [buffering guide](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide) covers every fix.

**No app for your TV:** use a Firestick or Android box instead.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I watch IPTV on a Samsung Smart TV?**
Yes — with IPTV Smarters Pro, SET IPTV or Smart IPTV from the Samsung store.

**Do I need a box?**
No, as long as your TV has a suitable app. A Firestick is only needed for older TVs or if you want TiviMate on a Samsung or LG.

**What speed does a Smart TV need?**
About 10 Mbps for HD and 25 Mbps for 4K.
