The Amazon Firestick is Canada's go-to IPTV device: cheap, plugs into any TV, and runs every major player. But which app you load matters more than people expect — it shapes how quickly channels change, how good the guide looks, and whether you can record or catch up.

Here's how the main Firestick IPTV apps stack up, and who each one suits.

## The apps side by side

| App | Price | TV guide (EPG) | Catch-up | Recording | Multiple playlists | Best for |
|-----|-------|----------------|----------|-----------|--------------------|----------|
| TiviMate | Free; paid Premium upgrade | Excellent, TV-style grid | Yes (Premium) | Yes (Premium) | Yes (Premium) | Everyday living-room viewing |
| IPTV Smarters Pro | Free | Good | Yes | Limited | Yes | Beginners and quick setup |
| GSE Smart IPTV | Free (ads) | Good | Yes | Yes | Yes | People who also use Apple |
| Kodi + PVR IPTV Simple Client | Free | Good once set up | Yes | Yes | Yes | Kodi users who like to tinker |

Check each app's store page for current pricing — premium upgrades are usually a small one-off or yearly fee.

## TiviMate — the best all-rounder

TiviMate looks and feels like a proper TV box. The guide is the familiar grid, channel switching is quick, and you can group favourites by category. The Premium upgrade adds catch-up, recording to a USB drive, multiple playlists and picture-in-picture. If you watch IPTV daily on a Firestick or Android TV, TiviMate Premium is the most comfortable experience there is.

**Setup in short:** install TiviMate (via the Downloader app or the Amazon Appstore where listed) → Add playlist → Xtream Codes → enter server, username, password → load the EPG.

## IPTV Smarters Pro — the easiest to begin with

Free, simple and on nearly every platform, so you can run the same app on your Firestick, phone and tablet. It splits Live TV, Movies and Series into clear sections and takes both M3U and Xtream Codes logins. The best pick for a first setup or a free trial.

**Setup in short:** open Smarters → Login with Xtream Codes API → enter any name plus server, username and password → channels load in under a minute.

## GSE Smart IPTV — the most flexible

GSE handles M3U, Xtream Codes, EPG files and external players, and it's a favourite with people who also use an iPhone or Apple TV, since it's available there too. The interface is busier than TiviMate's, but power users appreciate the control.

## Kodi with PVR IPTV Simple Client — for tinkerers

Kodi is a full media centre. With the PVR IPTV Simple Client add-on you can load an M3U playlist and EPG and get a capable TV interface. It takes longer to configure, so it's only really worth it if you already use Kodi.

## Installing IPTV apps on a Firestick

Some players aren't in the Amazon Appstore, so you install them with the free **Downloader** app:

1. On the Firestick go to **Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options** and enable **Install unknown apps** for Downloader (or **Apps from Unknown Sources** on older models).
2. Install **Downloader** from the Amazon Appstore.
3. Open Downloader and enter the download address from the app's official website.
4. Install the app, then delete the installer file to free up space.

Only ever download from official websites, and steer clear of "modded" builds, which can hide malware.

## Which should you choose?

- **Best TV experience:** TiviMate (Premium for catch-up and recording).
- **New to IPTV or on a trial:** IPTV Smarters Pro.
- **You use Apple gear too:** GSE Smart IPTV or Smarters, both on iOS and tvOS.
- **Already a Kodi user:** PVR IPTV Simple Client.

All of them work with an [IPTV Canada](/) subscription — we send both an M3U link and an Xtream Codes login, so you can switch apps whenever you fancy.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which Firestick is best for IPTV?**
The Fire TV Stick 4K or 4K Max — enough memory for big channel lists and 4K support. Older Lite models work but can feel sluggish.

**Is TiviMate free?**
The basic version is; Premium is a paid upgrade that adds catch-up, recording and multiple playlists.

**Why are channels slow to load?**
Clear the app's cache and switch to a wired connection — see our [buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide).
